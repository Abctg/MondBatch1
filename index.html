Portfolio level

Three things that only appear across all 55

A quarter of the fund yields under 1%

Dollar Tree, Workday and Fiserv pay nothing. Alphabet 0.25%, Thermo Fisher 0.30%, Meta 0.36%, Samsung 0.40%, Salesforce 0.69%, Microsoft 0.71%, CNH 0.86%, Alibaba 0.88%, Sony 0.89%.

That is 25.2% of the fund which a four-stage model with a zero-real terminal cannot value without assuming a payout policy change nobody has announced. Not an argument for selling any of them — an argument for the committee deciding explicitly what case it is holding them on.

The refinancing cycle reads three ways

Three long-duration contracted-rent businesses, three completely different interest-rate exposures, and none of it visible in a multiple.

Cellnex carries 4.4 years at a 2.1% average cost — the whole roll is ahead of it. VINCI carries 5.6 years at 4.5%, barely moved from 4.4% a year ago — already repriced. NNN REIT carries nearly 11 years — the roll never arrives inside the forecast window.

Six trailing P/Es are unusable

Merck & Co at 116.5x is acquired-IPR&D write-offs. Sony and DuPont at NM are spin-off accounting. Cellnex at NM is genuine losses. CNH at 46.7x is trough EPS. Workday at 42.2x is stock compensation and amortisation.

11.9% of the fund where a trailing-multiple screen returns the wrong answer, and in three cases the distortion is a corporate action rather than trading.
